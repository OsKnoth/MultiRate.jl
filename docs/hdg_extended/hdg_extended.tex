\begin{abstract}
Starting from the DGSEM with the LMARS flux, the interface momentum (the LMARS mass flux) is introduced as a new unknown
on the faces, as in hybridized DG. The system then consists of the element equations, in which the thermodynamic
variables see the faces only through the interface momentum, and an algebraic constraint that defines the interface
momentum. The constraint is relaxed: the time derivative of the interface momentum equals a relaxation parameter times
the constraint. In the fast system the tendencies of the thermodynamic variables then depend only on the momentum, inside
the element and on the faces.
\end{abstract}

\section{Fast system}
Variables $(\rho,\ m=\rho\mathbf u,\ \Theta=\rho\theta)$, coefficients frozen at a state (\code{docs/frozen\_dg}): $\theta$, $A=P'(\Theta)=\gamma p/\Theta$,
$c^2=A\theta$, pressure $p=A\Theta$:
\begin{equation}
\rho_t+\nabla\cdot m=0,\qquad \Theta_t+\nabla\cdot(\theta\,m)=0,\qquad m_t+\nabla p+g\rho\,\mathbf e_z=0 .\label{eq:fast}
\end{equation}
Thermodynamic variables $a=(\rho,\Theta)$, momentum $b=m$. With the pressure as additional variable (\code{DGPressureModel}) the
$p$ row is $A$ times the $\Theta$ row; it is not written separately.

\section{DGSEM with LMARS}
Tensor LGL nodes, collocated quadrature, element-local derivatives $\nabla_h$ (SBP), mass $J\,w$ per node. At a face node of
element $K$ with outward contravariant normal $\tilde n$, $s=|\tilde n|$, unit normal $n=\tilde n/s$, $m_n=n\cdot m$ and
\[
\sigma=\frac{s}{J\,w}\qquad(w\text{ the quadrature weight of the face node in the normal direction}).
\]
Interior face between the elements $L$ and $R$, $n$ pointing from $L$ to $R$, $\jump x=x_R-x_L$, $\avg x=\tfrac12(x_L+x_R)$, $c$ the frozen face
sound speed. The LMARS interface values (exact Riemann solution of the frozen normal acoustics) are
\begin{equation}
m^*=\avg{m_n}-\frac{\jump p}{2c},\qquad p^*=\avg p-\frac c2\jump{m_n};\label{eq:lmars}
\end{equation}
at walls (mirror state) $m^*=0$, $p^*=p+c\,m_n$. In strong form, at a face node of $K$ with the outward normal of $K$
($m^*$ taken with the sign of that normal),
\begin{align}
\rho_t&=-\nabla_h\cdot m-\sigma\,(m^*-m_n),\nonumber\\
\Theta_t&=-\nabla_h\cdot(\theta m)-\sigma\,(\theta_{\rm up}\,m^*-\theta\,m_n),\label{eq:dg}\\
m_t&=-\nabla_hp-g\rho\,\mathbf e_z-\sigma\,n\,(p^*-p),\nonumber
\end{align}
$\theta_{\rm up}$ the frozen upwind value; at interior nodes only the volume terms act. Because of the pressure jump in $m^*$ the
thermodynamic tendencies depend on the thermodynamic variables of both elements of a face: the mass flux of DGSEM-LMARS
couples $a$ with $a$.

\section{The interface momentum as additional variable}
On every face node introduce one unknown $\hat M$, the normal interface momentum (single-valued, with the orientation of
the face normal $n$). Replacing $m^*$ by $\hat M$ in the thermodynamic rows and adding the definition of $m^*$ as an algebraic
equation gives, as in hybridized DG,
\begin{align}
\rho_t&=-\nabla_h\cdot m-\sigma\,(\hat M-m_n),\nonumber\\
\Theta_t&=-\nabla_h\cdot(\theta m)-\sigma\,(\theta_{\rm up}\,\hat M-\theta\,m_n),\label{eq:hdg}\\
m_t&=-\nabla_hp-g\rho\,\mathbf e_z-\sigma\,n\,(p^*-p),\nonumber\\
0&=\avg{m_n}-\frac{\jump p}{2c}-\hat M\qquad(\text{interior faces}),\qquad \hat M=0\quad(\text{walls}).\label{eq:constraint}
\end{align}
The constraint \eqref{eq:constraint} is the transmission condition of the hybridized form. It is local to the face node and
can be solved for $\hat M$ explicitly; eliminating $\hat M$ gives back \eqref{eq:dg}. Conservation of mass and $\Theta$ holds for every
value of $\hat M$, since $\hat M$ is single-valued.

\section{Relaxation of the constraint}
The algebraic constraint is replaced by a relaxation equation: the time derivative of the interface momentum equals a
relaxation parameter $\kappa=1/\varepsilon>0$ times the constraint,
\begin{equation}
\hat M_t=\kappa\Big(\avg{m_n}-\frac{\jump p}{2c}-\hat M\Big),\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad \varepsilon\,\hat M_t=\avg{m_n}-\frac{\jump p}{2c}-\hat M .\label{eq:relax}
\end{equation}
The system \eqref{eq:hdg} with \eqref{eq:relax} is an ODE in the element unknowns and the interface momentum; for $\varepsilon\to0$
($\kappa\to\infty$) it tends to the DAE \eqref{eq:hdg}--\eqref{eq:constraint}, i.e.\ to DGSEM with LMARS.

\section{Structure}
With the element unknowns $a$, $b$, the face unknowns $\hat M$ and the forcing $f$ of the slow part, the fast system reads
\begin{align*}
a_t&=K\,b+R\,\hat M+f_a,\\
b_t&=L\,a+D_b\,b+f_b,\\
\varepsilon\,\hat M_t&=H_b\,b+H_a\,a-\hat M,
\end{align*}
with
\begin{itemize}
\item $K$: the volume divergence of $m$ (and $\theta m$) and the face terms $+\sigma m_n$, $+\sigma\theta m_n$ of the element's own momentum;
\item $R$: the face terms $-\sigma\hat M$, $-\sigma\theta_{\rm up}\hat M$, local to the face node;
\item $L$: pressure gradient, gravity and the central part $\avg p$ of $p^*$; $D_b$: the jump part $-\tfrac c2\jump{m_n}$ of $p^*$ (and $c\,m_n$ at walls);
\item $H_b$: $\avg{m_n}$, $H_a$: $-\jump p/(2c)$, both local to the two nodes of the face node pair.
\end{itemize}
The thermodynamic tendencies depend only on the element momentum $b$ and the interface momentum $\hat M$, i.e.\ in the fast
system only on momentum; they contain no thermodynamic variable. The pressure jump, which in DGSEM-LMARS couples $a$
with $a$, now enters only the equation of the interface momentum. The momentum tendencies depend on $a$ (pressure, gravity)
and on $b$ through $D_b$. Ordered as $(a,\ b,\ \hat M)$:
\[
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\\\hat M\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}0&K&R\\L&D_b&0\\\kappa H_a&\kappa H_b&-\kappa I\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\\\hat M\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}f_a\\f_b\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
On the gravity-wave grids the faces split into horizontal ($\xi$) and vertical ($\eta$, walls) ones; $\hat M$ on $\xi$-faces belongs to
the horizontal part, on $\eta$-faces and walls to the vertical part of a node column.
